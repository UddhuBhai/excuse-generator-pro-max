# 💀 Excuse Generator PRO MAX™

A completely unnecessary but highly advanced **Excuse Generator** built with **React**, **JavaScript**, **Framer Motion**, and JSON data.

Because accountability is overrated.

## 📖 About

**Excuse Generator PRO MAX™** is a fun interactive web application that generates excuses based on **what you messed up**, **who you're lying to**, and **how badly you're cooked**.

Choose a situation, select your intended victim, adjust the severity from **1–10**, and let the system generate an excuse along with completely scientific™ scores for:

* 🧠 Believability
* 🎨 Creativity
* 😭 Shamelessness
* 🚨 Chance of Getting Caught

The excuses are organized into different severity levels, ranging from **MILD INCONVENIENCE** all the way to **NUCLEAR**.

## 🚀 Features

* 💀 Generate excuses for different situations
* 🎯 Select who you're giving the excuse to
* 🌡️ Adjustable severity level from 1–10

## 🎮 How It Works

### 1. 💩 Choose Your Situation

Select what went wrong from situations such as:

* Didn't go to the gym
* Didn't study
* Didn't submit an assignment
* Was late
* Didn't reply to a message
* Didn't finish a project
* Missed a meeting
* Forgot something important
* Didn't do the work you promised
* Other

### 2. 🎯 Choose Your Victim

Choose who you're trying to explain yourself to:

* Professor
* Boss
* Parents
* Friend
* Partner
* Roommate
* Teammate
* Client
* Yourself
* The entire human race

### 3. 🌡️ Set Your Severity

Adjust the severity slider from **1 to 10** depending on how badly you messed up.

The application categorizes the severity into:

* 🙂 **MILD INCONVENIENCE**
* 😐 **PRETTY BAD**
* 😬 **THIS IS GETTING BAD**
* 💀 **I'M COOKED**
* ☢️ **NUCLEAR**

### 4. 🧠 Generate Your Excuse

The application selects an excuse based on your chosen situation and severity level, then displays it with an animated result card.

### 5. 📊 Receive Your Completely Scientific™ Scores

Every excuse gets scored based on your severity level:

* **Believability**
* **Creativity**
* **Shamelessness**
* **Chance of Getting Caught**

The scores are dynamically calculated with a combination of severity and randomization.
